System types

The right system for your situation

Not every property needs the same solution. We size and specify every system based on your actual consumption, your roof, and what you want to achieve — whether that's cutting your bill, going off-grid, or simply keeping the lights on during loadshedding.

Grid-tied solar

Panels feed directly into your property's electrical system, reducing what you draw from the grid. The most cost-effective entry point for solar — lower bills from day one. No battery required.

Battery backup systems

Store solar energy during the day and use it at night or during loadshedding. Essential for households and businesses that need consistent power regardless of the grid. Pairs with grid-tied or standalone.

Hybrid systems

The best of both — grid-tied with battery storage. Use solar during the day, draw from battery at night, and fall back to the grid only when needed. Maximum energy independence without going fully off-grid.

Commercial solar

Larger systems for business premises, farms, and industrial properties. We size systems to match actual business loads and structure the installation to satisfy COC and compliance requirements.

System assessments

Not sure what you need? We assess your current consumption, roof orientation, shading, and budget — and recommend a system that actually makes sense for your situation. No overselling.

COC certified installation

Every solar installation is completed by a registered electrician and issued with a Certificate of Compliance. Required by insurers and essential for resale value — not something every installer provides.

What we install

Proven inverter brands. No compromises.

The inverter is the heart of any solar system. We work exclusively with brands that have a proven track record in the South African market — supported locally, backed by real warranties, and built to handle our conditions.

International

Austrian engineering, global reputation. Fronius inverters are known for reliability, monitoring capability, and long operational life. A premium choice for grid-tied and hybrid systems.

Netherlands

The benchmark for off-grid and backup power systems. Victron's flexibility and monitoring tools make it the go-to for complex installations, farms, and applications that need reliable off-grid performance.
