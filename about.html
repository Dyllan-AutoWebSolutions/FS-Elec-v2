FS Energy Projects was built on the belief that electrical work should be done properly — not just to pass an inspection, but to last. Every installation we complete gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a DB board upgrade in a family home or a three-phase installation on a commercial site.

Based in Worcester, we've grown by doing good work and letting the results speak. Our clients range from homeowners wanting solar to reduce their bills, to industrial facilities that need a contractor who can handle the compliance side without hand-holding.
