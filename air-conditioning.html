What we cover

Installed right.
First time.

An aircon is only as good as its installation. Bad wiring, wrong sizing, or a poorly routed pipe run turns a good unit into a constant problem. We supply and install — so the electrical side and the mechanical side are handled by the same team.

Residential installation

Single and multi-split systems for bedrooms, living areas, and open-plan homes. We advise on the correct capacity for the room size and install neatly — cable runs concealed where possible.

Commercial installation

Office spaces, retail units, server rooms, and commercial kitchens. We size and install systems that handle the load and comply with building requirements. Multiple units coordinated as one job.

Supply & installation

We supply the unit and install it — one contractor, one quote, one point of accountability. No sourcing the unit yourself and hoping the installer turns up. We handle the full job.

Dedicated electrical circuits

Air conditioners need their own dedicated circuit — not shared with other appliances. We install the correct breaker, correct cable, and correct isolator as part of every installation.

COC on every installation

The electrical component of every aircon install is completed to COC standard. Important for insurance purposes and for commercial clients who need compliance records on all electrical work.

Sizing advice

Undersized units run constantly and never cool properly. Oversized units short-cycle and waste electricity. We recommend the correct BTU capacity for your space before anything is ordered.

Why have an electrician install your aircon?

Most aircon failures trace back to the electrical side — undersized cables, shared circuits, no isolator, or a breaker that trips under load. Because FS Energy Projects is a registered electrical contractor first, the wiring on every aircon installation is done properly. No shortcuts on the part most people never see.
